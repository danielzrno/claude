## Positioning

- **Name:** Mivada — from *"my village"* (community) + *wisdom* (knowledge). Core value: **We Care.**
- **Tagline:** *Technology, human first.*
- **One-liner:** Mivada is an Australian technology consultancy that turns enterprise
  platforms — Workday, payroll, data and AI — into measurable human outcomes.
- **Voice:** plain, confident, warm, specific. Short sentences, active voice, Australian
  English (organisation, optimise, centre). No hype words. Numbers over adjectives.

## Service pillars (4)

1. **Workday & ERP** — implementation, optimisation and managed support for Workday HCM, Financials and adjacent ERP.
2. **Payroll Consulting** — compliant, accurate payroll across complex awards and multi-entity structures.
3. **Data & AI** — Lakehouse architecture, governed analytics and applied AI on top of your people and finance data.
4. **Intelligent Automation** — combining RPA, ML and process design to remove low-value work.

## How we work — "Think human first"

A four-step engagement model:
1. **Listen** — understand the people and the work before the technology.
2. **Design** — shape the platform around how teams actually operate.
3. **Deliver** — implement in weeks-long increments, not multi-year programs.
4. **Care** — stay on after go-live; measure outcomes, not tickets closed.

## Data & AI spotlight (capability detail)

Certified consultants design **Lakehouse architectures**, build **Delta Lake pipelines**
(batch and streaming), and integrate **Databricks** with Workday, payroll and finance to
deliver **governed KPI stores** and **real-time insight** — so leaders decide on current data.

## Proof points (illustrative)

- Founded **2014** (formerly LJM Infotech)
- **120+** specialists
- Delivery **onshore in Australia + offshore in India**
- **40+** Workday deployments
- **98%** client retention
- **4** service pillars

## Differentiators ("Why Mivada")

1. **Certified specialists** — Workday- and Databricks-certified consultants, not generalists.
2. **Onshore + offshore** — senior onshore leadership with cost-effective India delivery.
3. **People-first change** — adoption built in from day one, so platforms actually get used.
