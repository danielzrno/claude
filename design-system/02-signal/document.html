capability statement  //  2026

Mivada is an Australian technology consultancy that turns enterprise platforms—Workday, payroll, data and AI—into measurable human outcomes. Founded in 2014, we pair certified specialists with a people-first delivery model: senior onshore leadership in Australia, cost-effective delivery in India, and adoption built in from day one. The name comes from my village and wisdom. Our core value is simple—we care.
