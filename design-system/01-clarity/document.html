Technology, human first.

Mivada is an Australian technology consultancy that turns enterprise platforms — Workday, payroll, data and AI — into measurable human outcomes. We pair senior onshore leadership with cost-effective offshore delivery, and we stay on after go-live to measure outcomes, not tickets closed. The name comes from my village (community) and wisdom (knowledge); our core value is that we care.
